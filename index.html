Clips

A personal-use tool that turns public Twitch highlight clips into vertical video and prepares them for posting to a single connected TikTok account.

What this is

Clips is built and used by one person, for one TikTok account. It is not a public product — there is no sign-up, no other users, and no accounts other than the single one its owner has connected.

The goal is straightforward: keep a personal content channel supplied with edited short-form video without doing every editing step by hand, while making sure nothing goes out publicly without a human looking at it first.

How it works

Source

Searches public Twitch clip listings for short highlight clips matching a fixed set of topics, and adds them to a local queue.

Build

Downloads the source clip, crops it to vertical format, and burns in a caption card. This step only ever touches files on the owner's own machine.

Combine

Groups a handful of related clips from the same source into one longer compilation, since a single short clip usually runs under a minute on its own.

Post as draft

Uploads the finished video to the connected TikTok account's own inbox as a draft, using the Content Posting API. It does not publish anything automatically.

The account owner makes the last call. Every video Clips prepares lands as a private draft in the connected account's own TikTok inbox. Nothing is posted publicly by the app — the owner opens TikTok, reviews the draft, and decides whether to publish it.

What it asks TikTok for

ScopeUsed for
user.info.basicConfirming which account is connected, nothing more.
video.uploadSending a finished video to that account's own draft inbox.

Clips never reads or acts on any account other than the one its owner explicitly connects, and never touches another TikTok user's data.

Source material

Clips only draws from publicly available Twitch clips — short highlight moments that are already public and shareable on Twitch's own platform. It doesn't scrape private or restricted content.
